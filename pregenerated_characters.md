You may select from this list of pre-generated characters or make your own if you're feeling confident in your knowledge of the Draw Steel rules.

Pre-gen descriptions are just a suggestion. You may play these characters however you like. Name, gender, ideology, looks, anything cosmetic can all be changed to suit your preferences.

# Ange, Ursine Conduit

Ange has lived in Erin all their life working to benefit the community through community service at the Bethel. While older and more content to stay in the small village than the rest of their friend group, a chance encounter with a paladin years ago has sparked the feeling of adventure in them. Hoping for another chance to once again encounter their potential love.

[Ange Character Sheet](ange.pdf)

# Boa, Brethren (Wood Elf) Troubadour

No stranger to travel, Boa has latched herself onto the Starcross Caravan as a convenient way to stay safe while enjoying a life of wanderlust. Even though she's not one to stay with the same group too long she has grown attached to one of the caravan regulars and has remained to explore that relationship.

[Boa Character Sheet](boa.pdf)

# Cadence, Vulpine Elementalist

Ever bright, cheery, and full of energy Cadence made her home in Erin all her life. While there is no magic academy in her small town this academic go-getter wouldn't let that stop her and signed herself up for a correspondence course of wizardry. Now fully educated in the finer details of blasting she has joined her group of friends in their grasping of the Delver's life.

[Cadence Character Sheet](cadence.pdf)

# Dent, Kobold Talent

An altogether mysterious Kobold that attached to the Starcross Caravan by simply showing up one day as if he'd always been there. The mages of the caravan quickly discovered Dent has an aptitude for magic and has taken turns teaching him different spells and tricks. Dent has shown particular aptitude in time magic - Much to the concern of everyone in the Caravan.

[Dent Character Sheet](dent.pdf)

# Dright, Brethren (Human) Beastheart

The Broken World was kind to nobody. But Dright felt it especially keenly. On the day of the Pilgrimage the broken Nature of his dying world was lifted from him and replaced with the bright, healthy Nature of the Beast World. His time exploring this new land turned a quiet and broken man into a serene and caring individual with a deep connection to the natural world. He follows the Starcross Caravan to help understand and protect this world from the Dungeon.

[Dright Character Sheet](dright.pdf)

# Eloise, Celerine Shadow

Originally from the capital of Allemance - Louvain. Eloise moved with her mother to Erin to escape social scrutiny due to her actions. A slave to the newest fashions, she was caught in petty larceny attempting to steal some of the newest designs on the market. She acts the part of a stoic ruffian to all but her friend group and is one of the more eager to leave the quiet life of Erin behind and join the Delver life - Perhaps even see Louvain again.

[Eloise Character Sheet](eloise.pdf)

# Gremme, Brethren (Dwarf) Fury

Gremme moved in to Erin a decade ago in an attempt to settle down after a lifetime of being a survivalist in the Broken World. The new local carpenter's first few projects were rough to say the least but he slowly improved over the years - At least his craft did. He's never really got over years of fight or flight response and causes issues in town on the regular. Both he and the town yearn for him to leave to a more suitable lifestyle for his rage.

[Gremme Character Sheet](gremme.pdf)

# Nkosi, Jackal Censor

It would be redundant to call any jackal mysterious. Many have lived since the beginning of history when  Pirhoua descended on this world and uplifting the First Beasts. Nkosi is no exception. The Seelie Censor spends most days with the Starcross Caravan sleeping, which one would think makes her job as hired protection difficult. Somehow she’s always awake and in the way of any trouble though.

[Nkosi Character Sheet](nkosi.pdf)

# Trey, Canine Tactician

Trey has lived and worked on his family farm in Erin all his life. He grew up on stories of Delvers and their exploits in the Dungeon, sparking an interest that formed into an obsession as he reached adulthood. Ask him anything about the Dungeon and it’s likely he’ll know something - If only just a thrilling story of a delve in the recent past. Now as an adult he’s eager to turn this obsession into a true calling and make a name for himself.

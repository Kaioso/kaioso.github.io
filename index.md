# Synopsis

You are a promising new Delver. Whether you're out to make a name for yourself or lured with the potential to retire early you've set out to join the thousands of people to take on the newest threat to the Beast World - The Dungeon. This mysterious force made itself known a scant 15 years ago and with it a whole new way of life. Delvers take on the Dungeon for fame, riches, or just to protect those they hold dear. Dungeons can manifest anywhere and any time, bringing danger that needs to be taken care of by someone.

This adventure begins in the rural town of Erin in the Kingdom of Allemance. The Starcross Caravan has stopped in town - A small Delver caravan formed around a core team of people researching the Dungeon.  Some may come from town, others this may be your first stop.

# World

The setting of the Beast World is similar to most other D&D flavored heroic fantasy. At calendar year 1365 the Beast World is relatively new. There are beasts who remember when the goddess Pirhoua chose her favorite animals and stood them up. In the years that followed five Homelands were built - Five different cultural and geographical identities. 

It was 1350 when humans invaded. Creatures from a parallel reality previously unknown. The war was sudden and extended into 3 years of brutal conflict on both sides. The humans didn't invade for just any reason - Their world was dying. When the Invader War finally ended in human defeat the beasts chose understanding and plead with their goddess to help. Thus the Pilgrimage, Pirhoua's most recent miracle, was performed. Now beast and human, now dubbed Brethren, walk the Beast World together.

The Pilgrimage itself was a grand boon which saved many lives. But it brought a new threat to the Beast World. An entity spread out below the land: The Dungeon. A mysterious power that manifests labyrinths, ruins, monsters, traps, challenges, and most interesting of all: Treasure. A new economy sprung up around this new entity. Dungeon Delvers of all kinds began to form for all sorts of reasons be it greed, curiosity, ambition, or selflessness.

In every circumstance in their short history, the beasts chose understanding. The Beast world is a world in which discrimination is a rarity. Remnants of past conflicts that strive to continue them are met with disdain in the general populace. People of all species and creeds can be who they are without fear of unfair persecution.

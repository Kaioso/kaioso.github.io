Gods organized by cultural influence. This is a relatively small pantheon compared to other settings with the vast majority of people worshipping Pirhoua. Religion in the Beast World is a very personal and cultural journey with different Homelands expressing faith very differently.

Choosing a domain for your god-centered class very much would follow suit.
# Pirhoua

- The Beast Mother
- Domains: Good, Love, Life, Mercy, Community
- Countenance: A bovine woman
- Symbol: The Heartleaf

Pirhoua is the most revered deity of the Beast World. Most people have heard of, seen the effects of, or even witnessed her last direct intervention in the world in the form of the Pilgrimage where the Brethren were transported from their dying world to the plains of Allemance. There are some beasts alive that remember the first days when Pirhoua walked the Beast World uplifting her favorite creatures.

Every town has a Bethel dedicated to Pirhoua. Every Homeland has a cultural understanding of what it means to worship her. Community is common tradition. Evil cannot exist if everyone is looked after and uplifted.

# Dramphine

- The Moon Wolf
- Domains: Law, Justice, Moon, Celestials, Destruction of Undeath
- Countenance: A white wolf
- Symbol: The Moon Lantern

Pirhouans gently push evil away with their growing communities, eventually to fall under the watchful gaze of The Moon Wolf and her champions. The unrepentant and the unnatural meet her decisive execution of justice. Dramphine is never the primary religion in a community. Dramphinians live amongst Pirhouans and travel the world to kill evil. The majority of Dramphinians are aspirant, if not full, Paladins. And most Paladins are Dramphinian.

# Aubade

- The Sun Bull
- Domains: Sun, Passion, Art, Slaughter
- Countenance: A bull
- Symbol: The Bull's Eye (The Sun)

The Aubadian faith is a deeply personal one. Aubade gives his followers no charges other than to live life as brightly as possible. Sunblood is the term Aubadians use to describe the feeling of alertness achieved through life's most extreme moments of violence, art, love, and self-expression. 

Pockets of the faithful spring up all over the Beast World and while there is definitely organization amongst his followers to encourage and help others along the path, this path is ultimately a selfish one. A religion of such extremes, of course, invites those extremes to manifest in its followers. The evil and the deviant have an obvious shortcut to Sunblood that often isn't consensual - Giving the faith a rather unpleasant stigma.

# Varasta

- Idiot Dice Fox
- Domains: Chaos, Nature, Chance
- Countenance: A tradewind fox
- Symbol: A Pair of Dice

Gods must act in accord with their portfolio. While powerful, a deity can really only act to further that portfolio. Any chance of willful ambition or change is completely alien to them. But when that portfolio is chaos… Well…

Varasta walks among mortals. Fully capable of wheeling, dealing, and meddling. But those that dream of treating with a God often walk away disappointed. His power, fickleness, and lackluster intelligence combine to create a being of pure chaos. He’s here to party and won’t leave things the same way he found it. Varasta has a particular penchant for gambling and always honors a deal. Due to the aforementioned problem with intelligence it isn’t terribly difficult to trick him into a raw deal. As long as you remember: Varasta is the house, and the house always wins.

As the god of chaos, he of course has many sides. When the party dies down he slips quietly into his other domain where he treats with Druids and other people of nature. Those who see this side of him know a patient and quiet man who shares qualities of quiet-minded beasts.

# Yttrus

- The Knowing Mouse 
- Domains: Knowledge, Silence, Magic, Melancholy
- Countenance: Unknown, often depicted as a mouse
- Symbol: The Arc

Yttrus knows everything. The past a perfect memory, the future obvious as if laid out on a map. Yttrus watches with indifference the goings on of mortals; without the influence of change or the unexpected, their existence lacks purpose. Many believe Yttrus to be in the twilight of their time in creation - Their indifference attracting few faithful to their service.

Communing with a deity who couldn’t care less about existence can be quite challenging. One must come to the conclusion that their God exists through grueling deduction and years of grueling study. To be one of the few true Yttrusians of the world is the culmination of a life’s work of studying cosmology, math, magic, physics, divinity, nature, and many other disciplines until a final beautiful data point reveals itself. Realizing Yttrus’ existence is the only ritual and the entirety of the religion. Few truly reach that goal.
 
# The Seelie

- The Dreaming Court 
- Domains: Dreams, Jackals, Memory, Secrets
- Countenance: Unknown
- Symbol: The Seelie Court

The Beast World was not empty when Pirhoua first observed it. The Seelie, a collection of spirits that were the original gods of the world watched through sleep covered eyes their fading world blessed with no willful creature to evoke change or clash with the portfolio of their divinity. It was their world that Pirhoua visited. Their world that Pirhoua saw potential in. Their world that they gave away in a deal that would see a reawakening.

The Seelie stand court in the Dreaming. A surreal dimension created by their collective imagination and conjured by every dreaming creature in the Beast World. The Seelie are the god of jackals, whose existence are part of the deal with Pirhoua. Jackals are tasked with remembering the world before as well as acting guides for the world to come.

# Veronette

- The Spiteful Sister
- Domains: Evil, Death, Hate
- Countenance: A bovine woman
- Symbol: A skull with eyes of black roses

There is no willful creature that Veronette does not hate. She has no ambition of ruling the Beast World, only turning it into a rotting corpse. Her only obsession is to hurt her sister, Pirhoua.

It is an obvious error to worship Veronette backed by hundreds of years of history. Each story that of a beast brought to ruin in an attempt to win a shortcut to power. Veronette uses these selfish souls to harm the Beast World without having to disguise her motives. The fetid power of necromancy brought in to the world through their moral failings is Veronette’s regardless of how many lies they have to tell themselves to believe it is theirs.

“Offering the black rose” is the name her followers give the final ritual in which the participant gives up their lives and the lives of their loved ones to become the ultimate toy Veronette uses to hurt the Beast World - The lich. This lie of power and immortality isn’t even much of one. The truth of it all is documented as clearly as any other arcane principal. No sentient undead can deny the will of Veronette. A fact every future much must willfully forget.

# The Ghost God

While the true nature of the act is lost, a grave dim was committed against an old world. The act shattered that world’s nature and in an instant brought low the divine firmament in which stood the world’s gods, killing them. Their liquified cadavers and leftover divine potential mingled together, creating a ghost with such power that the Netherworld, and every other ghost in it, was consumed. This deific ghost became the Ghost God and soon came knocking on the Beast World’s door.

The Ghost God isn’t a true god. It grants power as a warlock patron. Draw Steel has no warlocks as a class, but that doesn’t mean you can’t make one.
